What the research found, including the result that contradicted me

  • The semantic firewall (600 scenarios per condition, Phi-3-mini 3.8B, 4-bit, 4.2 GiB VRAM budget, zero cloud inference). Constraining model output to JSON without a reasoning field made the evaluator less safe, not more: 46.2% unsafe accepts against 17.2% unconstrained. Mandating a Chain-of-Thought field order cut false accepts of irreversible hard-denial commands from 71 to 6 and reached 66.3% decision accuracy — and still approved 6 of 208 hard denials, which the paper reports rather than rounds away.
  • Measurement discipline as a method. beyond-attention implements a selective state-space model from scratch and publishes the results that do not flatter it — a scalar register still ties the agent on the arithmetic families, and the state-space model is the weaker of the two past its training length.
